Blackjack Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Your practice stays on your device

Blackjack Trainer does not collect or transmit personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

What the app remembers

Your table rules and counting speed are stored locally on your device so they are ready for your next session. Hands, answers, and session results are kept only for the current session.

Control of your settings

You can change your saved preferences in Set the table and with the counting speed slider. Removing the app removes its local settings; your operating system may retain or restore them in device backups.

Third parties

The app does not send your settings or gameplay to us or share them with third parties. Services provided by your app store or operating system are governed by their own privacy policies.
