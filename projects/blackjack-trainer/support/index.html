Blackjack Trainer Support

Listed on the App Store as Blackjack Trainer & Counting

Blackjack Trainer checks every decision you make against the correct play for the exact table you set. It is a practice tool: there is no wagering, nothing to purchase, and nothing to win.

Questions, bugs, or suggestions: jacobkulberg@gmail.com

Changing the rules

Set the table, from the title screen, controls deck count, whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17, doubling after a split, resplitting aces, late surrender, and the blackjack payout. The house edge shown at the top updates as you change them, and the strategy the app checks you against is solved for whatever you have set — not a generic chart.

The counting drill

The drill uses the Hi-Lo system: add one for cards two through six, nothing for seven through nine, subtract one for tens, face cards, and aces. Cards come off the shoe at the speed set by the slider, and the count carries across the whole shoe rather than resetting between questions. Tap Pause, or press P, whenever you need to stop; leaving the app pauses the drill until you come back.

Your data

Nothing leaves your device. Your table rules and counting speed are saved locally so they are ready next time; hands and answers are not kept after a session. The full privacy policy has the detail.
